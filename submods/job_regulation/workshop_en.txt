[h2]Empire Job Statistics[/h2]

Macroeconomy: Job Statistics & Regulation provides a centralized view of jobs, workforce, and production across your empire.

[b]·[/b] Aggregates workforce totals for each job type
[b]·[/b] Calculates the precise current resource output of each job type from workforce and production modifiers
[b]·[/b] Displays monthly workforce changes, automation rate, and job output
[b]·[/b] Filters jobs by basic resources, industry, research, and strategic resources
[b]·[/b] Locates colonies that contain a selected job

[h2]Automatic Job Regulation[/h2]

Job regulation uses BCA's economic output targets and regulation tolerance to open or close relevant jobs, directing population toward the production sectors currently needed by your economy.

I recommend using [b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3171051580]Auto Resettle Pop[/url][/b] to redistribute population between colonies more quickly. Vanilla automatic migration also works, but usually reacts more slowly.

[h2]Enabling Automatic Job Regulation[/h2]

1. Subscribe to and enable Better Colony Automation.
2. Load Macroeconomy: Job Statistics & Regulation after Better Colony Automation.
3. Make sure Colony Automation is enabled on every colony that should participate.
4. Enable Job Management on those colonies. It is the first option in the colony automation menu.
5. Enable Economic Management in BCA's Global Automation Settings panel.
6. Enable Global Job Regulation in the same panel.

Economic Management and Global Job Regulation are disabled by default and must be enabled manually.

[h2]FAQ[/h2]

[b]Q: What if I only want job statistics and do not want the other features?[/b]
A: Disable Colony Automation and Global Job Regulation. The statistics page can still be used to inspect empire-wide jobs, workforce, and output.

[b]Q: What if I do not want colony automation, but still want job regulation?[/b]
A: Colony Automation must be enabled, but you can disable every colony automation option except Job Management on the colony.

[b]Q: What if I do not want a specific job to participate in regulation?[/b]
A: Each job can be configured independently in the job settings panel.

[b]Q: Is Auto Resettle Pop required?[/b]
A: No. Vanilla automatic migration also works, but workforce redistribution after regulation is usually slower.
