[h1]BCA & Ultimate Automation Compatibility Patch[/h1]

This patch keeps the lower-left global entries from Better Colony Automation and Ultimate Automation visible at the same time.

[b]Both of the following mods are required:[/b]

[list]
[*]Ultimate Automation
[*]Better Colony Automation: Automation Overhaul
[/list]

[h2]Load Order[/h2]

[list]
[*]Ultimate Automation
[*]Better Colony Automation: Automation Overhaul
[*]This compatibility patch
[/list]

Load this patch after both parent mods.

[h2]Compatibility Scope[/h2]

This release targets Ultimate Automation 2.7.*, Better Colony Automation 2.1.1, and Stellaris 4.4.*. The patch may need an update when either parent mod changes its global GUI.
