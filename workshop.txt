[h2]Still manually managing dozens of planets?[/h2]

Mid to late game in Stellaris often turns into a micromanagement nightmare:

[b]·[/b] Manually building on every single planet
[b]·[/b] Vanilla automation building randomly and even ruining your economy
[b]·[/b] Planet specialization requires tedious manual demolition and rebuilding

As your empire grows to dozens or even hundreds of planets, this quickly becomes overwhelming.

[h2]What does this mod do?[/h2]

✔ Automated construction [b]based on actual needs[/b] (not random building)
✔ Automatically removes unsuitable buildings, districts, and specializations for smooth planet transitions
✔ Smart resource control to prevent automation from crashing your economy
✔ Highly configurable, but works out-of-the-box
✔ Intuitive UI for planning and adjustments

[b]You set the direction — the system handles the execution.[/b]

[h2]Quick Guide[/h2]

1. [b]Enable automation[/b]: Turn on Planet Automation and configure options in the planet interface
2. [b]Planning & district setup[/b]: Use the new UI to configure mixed planning and district construction
3. [b]Global settings[/b]: Use the Global Automation Settings panel to configure empire-wide automation behavior

This mod improves almost all vanilla automation options, including pop assembly, housing, rare resources, crime management, etc. You can safely leave these options on by default.

[h2]Recent Feature Updates[/h2]

[b]· Stellaris 4.4 support[/b]: Updated automation for the changed 4.4 script APIs.
[b]· Nomad Arkship automation[/b]: Added support for the new Arkship designations, districts, zones, specialized buildings, and Passenger Dorm housing.
[b]· Building automation improvements[/b]: Construction priorities and demolition handling are now more consistent across designation-based and zone-based automation.

[h2]⚠️ Compatibility[/h2]

[b]It is strongly recommended to place this mod at the end of your load order[/b]

[b]·[/b] This mod primarily modifies vanilla automation logic and should not conflict with mods that do not touch automation
[b]✅ More building slot[/b]
[b]✅ Larger planet and more districts capacity[/b]
[b]⚠️⚠️ More zone slots[/b] would not build more zones and auto demolish would remove extra zones but it can be turned off

[b]⚠️ MOD buildings[/b] only automate for vanilla but would not break the mod
[b]🟥 MOD districts[/b] can't support, mod districts would break the mod
[b]⚠️ MOD zones[/b] only automate for vanilla but would not break the mod

This mod is compatible with Ultimate Automation,  but [b]must be loaded after it[/b] to override its changes. And ensure that Ultimate Automation's planet automation is off

[h1]Better Colony Automation (BCA) - 殖民地自动化改良 (4.4)[/h1]

[url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3601183337]4.2旧版本[/url]

反馈与交流：QQ 153870310

[h2]还在手动管理几十个星球？[/h2]

Stellaris 中后期往往会变成一场微操地狱：

[b]·[/b] 一个个星球手动建造
[b]·[/b] 自动托管乱建甚至拖垮经济
[b]·[/b] 想转型还要手动拆建筑

当帝国扩张到几十甚至上百个星球时，这些操作会迅速变成负担。

[h2]这个 MOD 能做什么？[/h2]

✔ 自动建设（基于需求，而不是无脑建造）
✔ 自动拆除不合适建筑、区划与特化，支持星球转型
✔ 智能控制资源，避免自动化拖垮经济
✔ 支持细致配置（但默认即可使用）
✔ 提供直观 UI 进行规划与调整

[b]你只需要为一个殖民地设定方向，剩下的交给系统。[/b]

[h2]指南[/h2]

1. [b]开启自动化[/b]：在行星界面开启【行星自动化】以及对相关自动化选项进行配置。
2. [b]混合规划与区划建造设置[/b] 现在已提供行星 UI 界面来设置混合规划和区划建造。
3. [b]全局设置[/b]：使用【自动建造全局设置】面板配置国家级自动化行为。

此 MOD 对原版的几乎所有自动化选项都进行了改良，包括人口组装、住房、稀有资源、犯罪度管理等，你可以放心让这些选项保持默认开启。

[h2]近期功能更新[/h2]

[b]· 支持 Stellaris 4.4[/b]：适配 4.4 版本变更的脚本 API。
[b]· 游牧方舟自动化[/b]：支持新增的方舟特化、区划、区划特化、强化建筑与乘客宿舍住房自动化。
[b]· 建筑自动化改进[/b]：统一按行星特化与区划特化进行建筑建设的优先级和拆除处理。
