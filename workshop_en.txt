[h2]Still manually managing dozens of planets?[/h2]

Mid to late game in Stellaris often turns into a micromanagement nightmare:

[b]·[/b] Manually building on every single planet
[b]·[/b] Vanilla automation building randomly and even ruining your economy
[b]·[/b] Planet specialization requires tedious manual demolition and rebuilding

As your empire grows to dozens or even hundreds of planets, this quickly becomes overwhelming.

[h2]What does this mod do?[/h2]

✔ Automated construction [b]based on actual needs[/b] (not random building)
✔ Automatically removes unsuitable buildings, districts, and specializations for smooth planet transitions
✔ Smart resource control to prevent automation from crashing your economy
✔ Highly configurable, but works out-of-the-box
✔ Intuitive UI for planning and adjustments

[b]You set the direction — the system handles the execution.[/b]

[h2]Quick Guide[/h2]

1. [b]Enable automation[/b]: Turn on Planet Automation and configure options in the planet interface
2. [b]Planning & district setup[/b]: Use the planet UI to configure mixed planning and district construction. Buildings are constructed according to the planet designation and the zones already built on that planet.
3. [b]Global settings[/b]: Use the Global Automation Settings panel to configure empire-wide automation behavior, including resource reserves, job thresholds, pre-construction, and economic management. Economic management limits related construction by output targets.

This mod improves almost all vanilla automation options, including pop assembly, housing, rare resources, crime management, etc. You can safely leave these options on by default.

[h2]Recent Feature Updates[/h2]

[b]· Output target thresholds[/b]: Configure target values for each output type in global settings; automation expands matching production only while income is below target, and this gate does not change colony plans. The vanilla Avoid Deficit automation option has been removed and replaced by output target controls.
[b]· Fixes and UX improvements[/b]: Settings now sync to built zones when zone auto-demolition is disabled, with fixes for several construction strategies and missing Nomad Arkship zone icons.

[h2]FAQ[/h2]

[b]Why does a planet not build anything even when I have enough resources?[/b]

Automatic construction requires all of the following:

1. [b]Mineral reserves are above the threshold[/b]: This threshold can be adjusted in the Global Automation Settings panel.
2. [b]Economic targets allow the construction[/b]: If economic management is enabled, the relevant monthly output must be below its target before automation expands that output.
3. [b]Free jobs are lower than the threshold[/b]: The job threshold can be adjusted in the Global Automation Settings panel, or relaxed by enabling pre-construction.
4. [b]Some bonus buildings can build earlier[/b]: Certain efficiency-boosting buildings are prioritized even while free jobs still exist.
