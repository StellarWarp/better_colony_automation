[h2]What does this mod provide?[/h2]

BCA reduces repetitive colony management by handling construction, demolition, transitions, and resource control from your plan.

✔ Automatically builds buildings, districts, and district specializations
✔ Automatically removes buildings, districts, and district specializations that no longer match the plan, supporting colony transitions
✔ Controls construction pace through mineral reserves, free jobs, and economic output targets
✔ Improves most vanilla colony automation options, including pop assembly, housing, rare resources, and crime management
✔ Provides a colony district settings panel and a global settings panel for planning and batch adjustments

[h2]What do you need to plan?[/h2]

BCA selects default district specialization preferences from the colony designation. Usually, [b]choosing the designation[/b] is enough.

For finer planning, you can manually adjust:

[b]·[/b] District specializations: change primary or secondary production directions
[b]·[/b] District counts: set how many districts of each type should be built
[b]·[/b] Demolition policy: whether BCA may remove buildings, districts, and district specializations that do not match the plan
[b]·[/b] Global rules: resource reserves, free-job thresholds, pre-construction, and economic output targets

Once configured, BCA executes the plan during monthly automation.

[h2]Quick Start[/h2]

1. [b]Enable Colony Automation[/b]: Turn on Planet Automation in the planet interface.
2. [b]Configure automation options[/b]: Keep what BCA should manage, disable what you want to handle manually.
3. [b]Check the colony district settings panel[/b]: Defaults follow the colony designation. For mixed planning, adjust district specializations and district counts.
4. [b]Set global rules[/b]: Use Global Automation Settings to configure resource reserves, free-job thresholds, pre-construction, economic management, and default auto-demolition behavior.
5. [b]Let time advance[/b]: BCA performs construction and demolition during the monthly automation flow.

If unsure, keep most defaults and set only the colony designation and district specializations first.

[h2]⚠️ Compatibility[/h2]

[b]It is strongly recommended to place this mod at the end of your load order.[/b]

[b]·[/b] This mod primarily modifies vanilla automation logic. It should not conflict with mods that do [b]not[/b] modify automation.
[b]·[/b] This mod relies on UI Overhaul Dynamic's district interface space to display the colony district settings panel.

[b]✅ More building slots[/b]
[b]✅ Larger planets and higher district capacity[/b]
[b]⚠️⚠️ More district specialization slots[/b] BCA will not build extra district specializations and may remove extras if district specialization auto-demolition is enabled.

[b]⚠️ Mod buildings[/b] Only vanilla buildings are automated.
[b]🟥 Mod districts[/b] Not supported. Extra district types may break automation behavior.
[b]⚠️ Mod district specializations[/b] Only vanilla district specializations are automated.

Compatible with Ultimate Automation if BCA loads after it and Ultimate Automation's planet automation is disabled.

To keep both mods' lower-left global entries visible, use the optional [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3775138672]BCA & Ultimate Automation Compatibility Patch[/url] and load it after both parent mods.

[h2]FAQ[/h2]

[b]Q: Will BCA automatically choose the best colony designation and district specializations for me?[/b]

A: No. The colony designation and its primary and secondary district specializations are part of your plan. BCA follows that plan.

[b]Q: Where is the global settings panel?[/b]

A: Use the persistent lower-left button in the main interface, the upper-left button in the colony district settings panel, or the Global Automation Settings edict.

[b]Q: I cannot see the colony district settings panel. What should I do?[/b]

A: Make sure UI Overhaul Dynamic is enabled. If another mod rewrites the district layout, the BCA panel may be pushed lower; scroll down in the district interface. A standalone colony settings panel is in progress.

[b]Q: Why does a planet not build anything even when I have enough resources?[/b]

A: Automatic construction requires:

1. [b]Mineral reserves above threshold[/b]
2. [b]Economic targets allow it[/b]: if enabled, monthly output must be below target.
3. [b]Free jobs below threshold[/b], unless pre-construction relaxes this.
4. [b]Some bonus buildings can build earlier[/b].

[b]Q: What if I only want BCA to build things, not demolish them?[/b]

A: Disable building, district, or district specialization auto-demolition. Defaults and bulk application are in global settings.

[b]Q: Why did my district specialization disappear?[/b]

A: District specialization auto-demolition may be enabled. BCA removes district specializations that do not match the panel plan. When disabled, built district specializations are synced back into the panel.

[b]Q: Can I only use job statistics or job regulation?[/b]

A: They are in the optional submod. For statistics only, disable Colony Automation and Global Job Regulation. For regulation, Colony Automation must be enabled, but other options can be disabled except Job Management.

[b]Q: Are buildings, districts, and district specializations from other mods supported?[/b]

A: Mainly vanilla content. More building slots and larger planets usually work. New district types are high-risk.
